# Glossary of Preferred Legal Diction

**(The tongue of the house — English primary; Spanish where natural for Paraguay)**

Use these words in covenants, articles, notices, and charges of office. Prefer them over the corporate stock phrases in the right-hand column of habit. Spanish equivalents are offered where they sit naturally beside the river and the *pueblo*; they are aids for bilingual drafting, not a second constitution.

---

## A

**Assembly** — The convened body that receives account, appoints and removes where empowered, and amends only by high bar. Not a “town hall,” not “all-hands,” not theater.  
*ES: asamblea.*

**Allocation** — The deliberate placing of common wealth, opportunity, or command. Critical allocations leave a record and require more than one hand.  
*ES: asignación / destinación de recursos comunes.*

---

## B

**Binding Principles** — The non-negotiable foundations of the Founding Word. Unerasable by ordinary or extraordinary corporate act.  
*ES: principios vinculantes / fundamentos no negociables.*

**Books** — Accounts, records, minutes, and evidences of allocation and command. Kept with honesty; open as transparency requires.  
*ES: libros y registros.*

**Breach of covenant** — Violation of the bond of the house, including undeclared conflict and concealed failure. Heavier than “contract default” in moral register; use when the Founding Word or Articles are touched.  
*ES: quebrantamiento del pacto / incumplimiento del convenio.*

---

## C

**Capital** — Money, vessels, skill, trust, or knowledge that enters the house — all of which must turn toward shared fruit. Not “dry powder,” not “AUM theater.”  
*ES: capital (en sentido amplio: bienes, oficios, confianza, saber).*

**Cause** — Stated grounds for ending a charge or removing power; particulars sufficient for defense and for the assembly’s understanding. Never a mask for convenience.  
*ES: causa justificada / justa causa.*

**Charge (of office)** — The written work owed, the standard of excellence, and the means by which failure becomes visible. Prefer over “job description,” “role,” or “KPIs.”  
*ES: cargo / encargo / cometido.*

**Chief steward** — The principal officer of day-to-day command. Removable. Not “CEO-as-brand,” not “founder-king.”  
*ES: administrador principal / primer administrador* (or statutory title required by Paraguayan form, tempered by this meaning).

**Common wealth** — Resources held for the mission and the people of the river, not as private ornament.  
*ES: bienes comunes / caudal común.*

**Conference (honest)** — First step in dispute: good-faith meeting without delay. Prefer over “escalation pathway.”  
*ES: conferencia de buena fe / diálogo honesto.*

**Conflict of interest** — Any interest that could bend allocation, oversight, or command toward self, kin, or ally. Must be declared *before* the act.  
*ES: conflicto de interés.*

**Corruption by silence** — Undeclared conflict; concealment that serves private gain. Named as such.  
*ES: corrupción por silencio / por omisión.*

**Covenant** — The preferred name for a binding undertaking of the house where spirit and obligation meet. Not a “contract of convenience.” Use *contract* when statute or counterparty form requires it; keep covenantal register in the house’s own undertakings.  
*ES: pacto / convenio* (cf. *contrato* when formulary demands).

---

## E

**Excellence** — Mandatory standard of the work of hands and mind. Shoddy work and quiet mediocrity are betrayal.  
*ES: excelencia (deber, no eslogan).*

**Exception (narrow, time-bound)** — The only lawful curb on default transparency; itself recorded as exception.  
*ES: excepción estrecha y temporal.*

---

## F

**Founding Word** — The covenant for river and people; supreme in spirit over these legal instruments. Not amendable by corporate act.  
*ES: Palabra Fundacional.*

**Fruit / shared fruit** — Yield of capital and labor turned toward common strengthening. Prefer over “upside,” “carry,” or “EBITDA theater.”  
*ES: fruto / fruto compartido.*

---

## H

**House** — Electrozavod as moral and institutional whole. Prefer over “firm,” “platform,” “vehicle,” or “NewCo.”  
*ES: casa* (and, when naming the enterprise in trade: *la Naviera*).

---

## K

**Knowledge sharing** — Default lean toward openness where sharing does not cause harm. Prefer over “IP maximalism” as ideology.  
*ES: compartir el saber / apertura del conocimiento.*

---

## L

**Light** — Metaphor of default transparency: acts open to scrutiny. “Open to the light” is preferred diction.  
*ES: luz / a la luz del día.*

---

## M

**Mission** — The enduring purpose of Article II / the Founding Word; outlasts any steward.  
*ES: misión / propósito perdurable.*

---

## N

**Naviera** — Shipping house upon the river; Electrozavod’s vocation in truth, not only in name. Keep the Spanish in English texts when naming the vocation.  
*ES: Naviera (de los paraguayos).*

**No sacred persons** — Founders and officers stand under equal measure with the least participant. Non-negotiable.  
*ES: no hay personas sagradas / nadie está por encima de la regla.*

---

## O

**Office** — A constrained, visible, removable seat of power. Never a throne.  
*ES: cargo / oficio.*

**Ornament of power** — Capital, title, or display retained for vanity or domination rather than shared fruit. Forbidden as end-state.  
*ES: ornamento del poder.*

---

## P

**People / pueblo** — Those for whom the house exists; the Paraguayan people and those who share the life of the river. Prefer over “stakeholders” when the Founding Word’s beneficiary is meant.  
*ES: pueblo / el pueblo paraguayo.*

**People over profit** — Binding priority. Profit is servant, never master.  
*ES: el pueblo sobre el lucro / la gente antes que la ganancia.*

**Plurality of hand** — More than one person required for critical acts of allocation, ownership, or command. Anti-corruption by form.  
*ES: pluralidad de manos / más de una mano.*

**Power (constrained, visible, removable)** — The only legitimate form of command in the house.  
*ES: poder limitado, visible y removible.*

**Purpose** — Article II end: movement of goods serving the people’s strength, knowledge, and freedom.  
*ES: propósito / fin.*

---

## R

**Reckoning** — Accountability process for breach: censure, restitution, removal, remedy. Prefer over “HR process” when covenant is engaged.  
*ES: rendición de cuentas / ajuste de cuentas (en sentido de justicia, no de venganza).*

**Removable power** — Office that can be taken back by assembly or oversight body for stated grounds.  
*ES: poder removible / destituible.*

**Resilience** — Refusal to trust a single path, ally, or strength alone. Prefer over “de-risking” jargon.  
*ES: resiliencia / fortaleza durable.*

**River** — The Paraguay and the life of waters that the house serves; older than names. Keep the elevated sense in preambles; use precise geographic names in operational schedules.  
*ES: el río / el río Paraguay.*

---

## S

**Steward / stewardship** — Holder of title or office as bearer of duty before claim. Prefer over “owner” when speaking of moral posture; keep “shareholder” / *accionista* where statute requires.  
*ES: administrador / custodio / mayordomo* (choose by context; *administrador* often fittest in formal ES).

**Succession (by rule)** — Advance-fixed path for vacancy; not scramble, not dark bargain.  
*ES: sucesión por regla / sucesión reglada.*

**Surplus** — Yield available after endurance and excellence of the house are secured; allocated by written rule. Prefer over “free cash flow” when speaking in covenant. Use accounting terms in the schedules where auditors require them.  
*ES: excedente / superávit.*

---

## T

**Transparency (default)** — Openness as the ordinary state; secrecy the narrow exception.  
*ES: transparencia por defecto / como regla.*

---

## U

**Unerasable** — Quality of Binding Principles and of the Founding Word’s hard lines; beyond amendment that would hollow them.  
*ES: imborrable / no eliminable.*

---

## W

**Word** — Short name for the Founding Word when context is clear.  
*ES: la Palabra (Fundacional).*

**Witness / whistle** — Bringing breach to light; protected against retaliation.  
*ES: denuncia de buena fe / testimonio; protección al denunciante.*

## Words to Prefer Less (or Only When Statute Demands)

| Corporate habit | House preference |
|-----------------|------------------|
| firm / vehicle / NewCo | house / Naviera / Electrozavod |
| CEO / founder-brand | chief steward / steward of charge |
| KPIs / OKRs (as ideology) | charge of office / work owed / standard required |
| EBITDA theater / upside | surplus / fruit / shared fruit |
| stakeholders (vague) | people / pueblo / those who share the life of the river |
| terminate for convenience (casual) | end for cause stated; or mutual release with reckoning |
| best-in-class governance | anti-corruption by form; power constrained, visible, removable |
| entire agreement (as eraser) | this Covenant with the Founding Word and Articles; no side letter undoing the Word |
| alignment / incentives | just return under honest covenant; excellence mandatory |
| confidential (default maximal) | kept from harm where needed; lean to the light otherwise |

## Drafter's Bilingual Note

In instruments for Paraguay: use the statutory Spanish titles and formulary the Republic requires (*estatutos*, *asamblea de socios/accionistas*, *administrador*, *Naviera*, registrations before the competent authorities). Beside that formulary, keep the covenantal diction — *pacto*, *pueblo*, *fruto compartido*, *poder removible*, *no hay personas sagradas* — so that the spirit remains audible. Guaraní may be used in teaching and assembly so that none may say the Word was hidden; fix a language of record for formal acts as the Articles provide.

---

## Closing

Diction is not decoration. The wrong word softens a hard principle; the right word keeps the blade true.

Choose as the Founding Word chooses: for the living, for those yet unborn, and for the dignity of the people upon the river.
