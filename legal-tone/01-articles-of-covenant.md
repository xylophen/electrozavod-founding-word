# Articles of Covenant of Electrozavod

**(A Constitution of Stewardship for the House upon the River)**

These Articles give institutional form to the Founding Word. They are not a substitute for instruments drawn by counsel admitted in the Republic of Paraguay, nor for the maritime and commercial statutes that bind a Naviera. They are the measure by which such instruments must be judged: if a formal text contradicts these Articles in spirit, the formal text must yield or be remade.

Let none who hold office claim ignorance of what follows.

---

## Article I — Of the Name and the Nature

**1.1** The house is called Electrozavod. It is raised to be the Naviera of the Paraguayans — not in name only, but in truth.

**1.2** Electrozavod is a covenant of work upon the waters and upon the shore that serves them: a steward of vessels, knowledge, trust, and common wealth, ordered toward the enduring good of the Paraguayan people and of those who share the life of the river.

**1.3** It is not a private instrument of extraction. It is not a throne for any founder, investor, or officer. It is a house whose mission endures beyond any person who holds charge within it.

---

## Article II — Of the Purpose

**2.1** The first charge of the house is this: that the movement of goods upon the waters shall serve the increase of the people's strength, their knowledge, and their freedom.

**2.2** Profit is the servant, never the master. Where the two contend, the good of the people shall prevail.

**2.3** The house holds that every person bears inherent dignity; that rules must be written, equal, and binding upon the high as upon the low; that truth is to be sought without fear; and that excellence in the work of the hands and of the mind is a duty owed to the living and to those yet unborn.

**2.4** No amendment, investor preference, or side undertaking may invert this purpose. Capital that cannot accept this subordination shall not enter the house.

---

## Article III — Of Ownership and Stewardship

**3.1** Those who hold title, share, or beneficial interest are stewards of a charge, not owners of a people. Title confers duty before it confers claim.

**3.2** Every form of capital that enters this house — whether of money, of vessels, of skill, of trust, or of knowledge — must in time be turned toward shared fruit and toward the strengthening of the people. Nothing may remain as mere ornament of power.

**3.3** Transfers of controlling interest shall be visible to the assembly and subject to the constraints of these Articles and of the Founding Word. Secret control is forbidden.

**3.4** Founders stand under the same measure as the least participant. There are no sacred persons. Founding status confers no immunity from removal, reckoning, or equal application of the rules.

---

## Article IV — Of Power: Constrained, Visible, Removable

**4.1** Power within this house is legitimate only while it serves the purpose of Article II.

**4.2** Every office of command — including the chief steward and any body that allocates capital or ownership — shall be:
- **Constrained** by written charge, by these Articles, and by the Founding Word;
- **Visible** in its acts of allocation, appointment, and discipline, through books and records open as Article VI requires;
- **Removable** by the assembly or by the body charged with oversight, upon grounds of breach, corruption, sustained failure of excellence, or betrayal of the purpose.

**4.3** No office shall be structured so that a single hand may seize what belongs to the many without the knowledge of the many. Critical acts of allocation, ownership, and command require plurality of hand or contemporaneous record sufficient for later scrutiny.

**4.4** The mission outlasts the steward. Vacancy, incapacity, or removal shall trigger succession by rule, not by scramble or by private bargain in the dark.

---

## Article V — Of the Assembly

**5.1** There shall be an assembly of those who bear standing under the formal instruments of the house — stewards of title, holders of defined charge, and such others as the formal bylaws admit — convened to receive account, to appoint and remove where empowered, and to amend only as Article X allows.

**5.2** The assembly is not theater. It is the place where power answers. Notices shall be timely; materials shall be sufficient for judgment; silence shall not be manufactured by exhaustion or by fog.

**5.3** Guaraní and the tongues of the West may be used so that none may say the Word or these Articles were hidden. Formal acts shall be recorded in a language of record fixed by the house, with faithful translation where the people of the work require it.

---

## Article VI — Of Transparency

**6.1** Transparency shall be the default. What is done with the common wealth, the common ownership, and the common hope must be open to the light.

**6.2** Narrow exception is permitted only where clear and temporary necessity of safety, lawful confidentiality of third parties, or fair dealing in active negotiation requires otherwise. Every such exception shall be time-bound, narrowly drawn, and itself recorded as an exception.

**6.3** Books and records of the house — accounts of vessels and cargoes where the house controls them, allocations of surplus, related dealings, and the minutes of command — shall be kept with honesty and made available to those entitled under the formal instruments and under law.

**6.4** Concealment that serves private gain or quiet domination is breach of covenant.

---

## Article VII — Of Conflict of Interest and Anti-Corruption by Form

**7.1** Corruption shall be made difficult by the very form of the work, not merely by exhortation after the fact.

**7.2** No steward, officer, or person with charge shall allocate common wealth, opportunity, or command to self, kin, ally, or controlled entity without prior declaration and without the approval required by the formal instruments — which approval shall never rest in the interested hand alone.

**7.3** Undeclared conflict is corruption by silence. Gifts, favors, and side bargains that bend allocation or oversight are forbidden.

**7.4** Whistle and witness shall be protected. Retaliation against those who bring truth to light concerning breach of these Articles is itself breach of the highest order.

---

## Article VIII — Of the Allocation of Capital and Surplus

**8.1** Surplus and fruit of the common work shall be allocated by written rule, not by the mood of the powerful.

**8.2** The order of care shall be:
1. the endurance, safety, and excellence of the house and its vessels and people;
2. the just return owed under covenants honestly made;
3. the shared strengthening of those who labor in the house and of the people of the river — teaching, skill, and capacity;
4. only thereafter, distributions that do not starve the foregoing.

**8.3** No distribution or extraction shall be structured to evade Article II or to ornament private power while the house weakens.

**8.4** Knowledge that can be shared without harm shall lean toward openness. Tools of governance and of the work shall prefer forms that many hands may improve.

---

## Article IX — Of Excellence and Failure

**9.1** Excellence is not optional. Shoddy work, hidden failure, and the quiet acceptance of mediocrity are betrayals of the covenant.

**9.2** Each charge of office shall state the work owed and the standard required. Failure shall be brought to light without delay, that remedy may begin.

**9.3** Resilience is required. No single path, single ally, or single strength shall be trusted alone. The house shall prefer durable competence to brittle brilliance that cannot survive the storm.

**9.4** Honest failure met with candor may be forgiven and taught from. Concealed failure is cause for removal.

---

## Article X — Of Amendment and the Unerasable

**10.1** These Articles may be amended only by a high bar set in the formal instruments — a supermajority of the assembly (or equivalent under Paraguayan form), with prior notice, written text, and recorded vote.

**10.2** No amendment may erase, invert, or hollow out the Binding Principles of the Founding Word, including without limitation: people over profit; transparency as default; anti-corruption by form; capital turned to shared fruit; knowledge sharing without harm; resilience; power constrained, visible, and removable; excellence mandatory; no sacred persons; founders bound as the least.

**10.3** Any purported amendment that contradicts Article 10.2 is void in the covenant of this house, whatever ink it bears, and shall be treated as breach.

**10.4** The Founding Word itself is not amendable by ordinary or extraordinary corporate act. It may be translated, published, and taught. It may not be rewritten to bless what it forbade.

---

## Article XI — Of Breach and Remedy

**11.1** Breach of these Articles or of the Founding Word by one who holds charge is cause for reckoning: censure, restitution, removal, and such civil remedy as law allows.

**11.2** The house shall not trade silence for comfort. Where breach touches corruption or the making of sacred persons, remedy shall be public within the assembly's lawful bounds.

**11.3** Settlement may spare humiliation that serves no truth; it shall not spare accountability that truth requires.

---

## Article XII — Of Interpretation

**12.1** These Articles shall be read in the light of the Founding Word. Where doubt arises, the reading that better serves the people's enduring good, the openness of the books, and the removability of power shall prevail over the reading that serves private convenience.

**12.2** Headings are for guidance. The operative text binds.

**12.3** Counsel admitted in Paraguay shall render these Articles into the forms required by the Republic's law — sociedades, maritime registration, labor, and tax — without betraying their spirit. Where statute mandates formulary words, those words shall stand beside, not instead of, the covenantal undertakings of this house.

---

## Closing

Let these Articles be read when office is conferred, when surplus is allocated, when dispute arises, and when amendment is proposed.

If in later days the house forgets them and becomes only another instrument of private gain or of quiet domination, it will have broken faith with the Founding Word — and its name will become a reproach.

But if it holds, even through difficulty, then those who come after may say that upon the open vein of the continent a people not only remembered their dignity, but bound their power accordingly.

These are the Articles of Covenant of Electrozavod.

Spoken for the days that are coming.
