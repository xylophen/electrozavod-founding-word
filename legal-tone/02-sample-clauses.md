# Sample Clauses — Rewrite Pack

**(Dry modern corporate → Electrozavod legal tone)**

These specimens teach the tongue. They are not plug-and-play substitutes for counsel admitted in Paraguay, nor for mandatory formulary under maritime convention or statute. Use them to retune drafts: keep the legal effect where sound; change the register and harden the principles where corporate habit has gone soft or opaque.

---

## 1. Force Majeure

### BEFORE
> Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement if such failure or delay results from circumstances beyond the reasonable control of that party, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, embargoes, acts of civil or military authorities, fire, floods, accidents, strikes, or shortages of transportation, facilities, fuel, energy, labor, or materials. The affected party shall notify the other party within five (5) business days and shall be excused for the duration of the force majeure event.

### AFTER
> When flood, war, plague, embargo, fire, or other force beyond honest foresight and beyond the obligated party's true command renders performance impossible — not merely inconvenient — that party shall give prompt written notice, set forth the nature of the hindrance, and mitigate as a steward must. Obligation is suspended only while the impossibility endures, and only to the extent it endures. Calamity shall not be used as a cloak for neglect, for under-provision, or for the quiet abandonment of excellence. When the way opens, performance shall resume without delay. If the hindrance persists beyond a time fixed in the particular covenant, either party may seek honest release or renegotiation — never a one-sided escape that leaves the weaker bearing all the ruin.

## 2. Confidentiality

### BEFORE
> The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party. Confidential Information means all non-public information, whether technical, commercial, or financial, disclosed by either party. Obligations shall survive for five (5) years after termination. This clause shall not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party.

### AFTER
> What must be kept from harm — trade that would wound fair dealing, personal dignity, safety of vessels and crews, or lawful confidence of a third party — shall be kept, and kept with care. What may be shared without harm shall lean toward the light; secrecy is a narrow gate, not a habit of rule. No covenant of silence shall be drafted to bury corruption, to hide allocation of common wealth, or to forbid witness of breach of the Founding Word or the Articles. Upon ending of the particular bond, confidences that still protect from harm remain; confidences that serve only private fog do not. Public truth honestly obtained is not a breach.

---

## 3. Dispute Resolution

### BEFORE
> Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be finally settled by binding arbitration administered by [Institution] in accordance with its rules. The seat of arbitration shall be [City]. The language shall be English. Judgment upon the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL AND TO PARTICIPATE IN CLASS ACTIONS.

### AFTER
> Dispute shall first be met with honest conference, in good faith and without delay, between those who hold charge. If conference fails, the parties shall submit to a forum that is fair, visible in its essential process, and not designed to exhaust the weaker party by cost or distance. No seat, language, or procedure shall be chosen to bury truth or to place reckoning beyond the reach of those who bear the consequences. Where arbitration is used, the award shall be reasoned enough that the assembly of the house, where concerned, may understand what was decided and why. Nothing herein waives the house's duty to reckon internally with corruption or with breach of the Articles.

## 4. Conflict of Interest

### BEFORE
> Directors and officers shall avoid conflicts of interest. Related-party transactions that are material must be approved by a majority of disinterested directors. Failure to disclose a conflict may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, subject to applicable law.

### AFTER
> No steward, officer, or person with charge shall allocate common wealth, opportunity, command, or surplus to self, kin, ally, or any entity under their influence by shadow. Conflict shall be declared in writing *before* the act. Approval shall never rest in the interested hand alone; plurality of disinterested judgment is required, and the declaration and decision shall enter the record open to lawful scrutiny. Undeclared conflict is corruption by silence. Retaliation against those who bring such silence to light is breach of the highest order. The founders are bound as tightly as the least participant. There are no sacred persons.

---

## 5. Profit Distribution / Surplus

### BEFORE
> Subject to applicable law and lender covenants, the Board may declare dividends from available EBITDA at its sole discretion. Management incentive compensation shall be aligned with annual EBITDA targets and strategic KPIs as approved by the Compensation Committee.

### AFTER
> Surplus and fruit of the common work shall be allocated by written rule, not by the mood of the powerful. First: the endurance, safety, and excellence of the house, its vessels, and its people. Second: the just return owed under covenants honestly made. Third: the shared strengthening of those who labor and of the people of the river — teaching, skill, and capacity. Only thereafter: distributions that do not starve the foregoing. No extraction shall be dressed as "alignment" while the house weakens, books fog, or excellence is traded for theater. Charges of office name the work owed and the standard required; they are not ornaments of private brand.

## 6. Termination for Cause

### BEFORE
> The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately for Cause, which includes (i) material breach, (ii) gross negligence or willful misconduct, (iii) conviction of a felony, (iv) fraud or embezzlement, or (v) failure to perform duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day cure period. The Company may also terminate without Cause upon sixty (60) days' notice, subject to severance as set forth in Schedule A.

### AFTER
> The house may end a charge or covenant *for cause* where there is: material breach of the bond; corruption or undeclared conflict; concealed failure; sustained refusal of excellence after written warning and a fair chance to amend; or betrayal of the purpose of the Founding Word. Cause shall be stated in writing with particulars sufficient for defense and for the assembly's understanding. Convenience alone — the wish of a powerful hand to be rid of an inconvenient truth-teller — shall not wear the mask of cause. Where the formal instruments allow ending without cause, the house shall reckon the harm and the duty owed, and shall not use silence as the price of departure. Removal of power remains always available where Article IV of the Articles of Covenant is engaged.

## 7. Books and Records

### BEFORE
> The Company shall maintain books and records in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles. Shareholders of record may inspect the stock ledger and certain books during normal business hours upon five (5) business days' written notice, for a proper purpose, subject to reasonable confidentiality restrictions.

### AFTER
> Transparency is the default. Books and records of accounts, vessels and cargoes where the house controls them, allocations of surplus, related dealings, appointments, removals, and minutes of command shall be kept with honesty and made available to those entitled under the formal instruments and under the law of the Republic. Narrow exception — safety, lawful third-party confidence, fair dealing in active negotiation — shall be time-bound, narrowly drawn, and itself recorded as exception. Inspection shall not be frustrated by fog, delay, or the invention of improper purpose where the asker seeks what the covenant already promises: light upon the common wealth. Concealment that serves private gain is breach.

---

## 8. Succession of Office

### BEFORE
> In the event of the CEO's death, resignation, or inability to serve, the Board shall appoint an interim CEO and commence a search for a successor at its discretion. The CEO may designate an emergency successor subject to Board ratification.

### AFTER
> The chief steward holds removable power, not a throne. Upon death, resignation, incapacity, or removal, succession follows rule fixed in advance — interim charge, then appointment by the body empowered under the Articles — not scramble, not private bargain in the dark, not hereditary claim. Any emergency designation by the departing steward is provisional only and dies if the assembly or oversight body withholds confirmation. The mission endures beyond the person. No succession plan shall create a sacred person or place any founder beyond the equal measure of the rules.

## Drafter's Note

When rewriting a full instrument:

1. Keep mandatory statutory and convention language where the law of Paraguay or the applicable maritime regime demands it.
2. Retune the house's own undertakings into this register.
3. Strike any clause that makes a sacred person, hides a hand, or sets profit above the people.
4. Prefer plurality of hand and contemporaneous record for critical acts of allocation, ownership, and command.
5. Cross-reference the Founding Word and the Articles where the bond touches purpose, surplus, power, or amendment.

These specimens serve the Charter of the Legal Voice. They do not replace counsel.
