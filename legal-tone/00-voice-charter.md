# Charter of the Legal Voice

**(How this house speaks when it binds, forbids, and reckons)**

The Founding Word declares *why* Electrozavod stands. This Charter declares *how* it must speak whenever it puts ink to obligation — in covenants, bylaws, notices, charges of office, and the settlement of dispute.

Legal speech is not lesser speech. It is the Word made operable. Therefore it shall keep the same register: solemn, elevated, wise, covenantal. It shall never borrow the tongue of the marketplace that flatters, nor the tongue of the bureaucracy that hides, nor the tongue of the parody that softens hard truth with costume.

These pages are instruments of tone and substance for counsel admitted in Paraguay to formalize under the law of the Republic. They do not replace that counsel. They bind those who draft on behalf of the house to a manner worthy of the covenant.

---

## Of the Register

**Sound like this:** counsel given at the edge of winter — measured, clear, without ornament that serves vanity, without softness that serves comfort. Speak as one who knows that words outlast the speaker, and that a false clause is a seed of later shame.

**Never sound like this:** the pitch deck, the compliance theater, the brand manifesto, the cute fantasy pastiche, the corporate apology that names no fault. Do not write as if the reader were a consumer to be managed. Write as if the reader were a partner under oath, or an adversary who will one day read the same page in daylight.

Hard principles stay hard. People over profit; transparency the default; corruption made difficult by form; capital turned to shared fruit; knowledge shared without harm; resilience preferred to brittle strength; power constrained, visible, and removable; excellence mandatory; no sacred persons; founders bound like the least. The legal tongue may clarify these. It may not dilute them.

---

## Of What This Voice Serves

1. **Clarity before cleverness.** Ambiguity that favors the powerful is a form of quiet domination. Prefer plain elevation to clever fog.
2. **Form that resists capture.** Clauses shall be shaped so that critical acts leave a trail, require more than one hand, and can be examined by those who bear the consequences.
3. **Equality before the rule.** Titles of office do not lighten the burden of the rule. The founder and the deckhand stand under the same measure.
4. **Continuity of the mission.** The house outlasts any steward. Language of succession, removal, and amendment must protect the mission more fiercely than it protects any person.

## Do and Do Not — Concrete Examples

| # | Do not (corporate / soft / pastiche) | Do (Electrozavod legal voice) |
|---|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | "Maximize shareholder value subject to ESG considerations." | "Profit is the servant of the people's enduring good. Where profit and that good contend, the good of the people shall prevail." |
| 2 | "Key Performance Indicators shall be cascaded to business units." | "Each charge of office shall name the work owed, the standard of excellence required, and the means by which failure becomes visible." |
| 3 | "The Company reserves the right to terminate for convenience." | "The house may end a covenant for cause stated in writing, or by mutual release; convenience alone shall not undo what was sworn without reckoning the harm." |
| 4 | "Confidential Information includes all non-public proprietary data." | "What must be kept from harm shall be kept; what may be shared without harm shall lean toward the light. Secrecy is a narrow gate, not a habit of rule." |
| 5 | "Force majeure excuses performance for Acts of God and similar events." | "When flood, war, plague, or other force beyond honest foresight renders performance impossible, the obligated party shall give prompt notice, mitigate as a steward must, and resume when the way opens — without using calamity as a cloak for neglect." |
| 6 | "Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in [venue]." | "Dispute shall first be met with honest conference. Failing that, by means that are fair, visible, and not designed to exhaust the weaker party. No forum shall be chosen to bury truth." |
| 7 | "The CEO shall have full authority to run the business day-to-day." | "The chief steward holds removable power, constrained by the Articles, visible in the books, and answerable to the assembly. No office is a throne." |
| 8 | "Related-party transactions require Board approval if material." | "No steward shall allocate common wealth to kin, ally, or self by shadow. Conflict of interest shall be declared before the act; undeclared conflict is corruption by silence." |
| 9 | "We're committed to integrity and best-in-class governance." | "Corruption shall be made difficult by the form of the work. Critical acts of allocation, ownership, and command require more than one hand and leave a record open to lawful scrutiny." |
| 10 | "Let's embark on this epic journey together, fellowship-style!" | *(Refuse the pastiche.)* "We take up this work under the Founding Word, bound as the least is bound, that the river may bear life and not only burden." |
| 11 | "EBITDA targets and incentive alignment for the C-suite." | "Surplus and fruit of the common work shall be allocated by rule: first to the endurance and excellence of the house, then to shared strengthening of those who labor and of the people of the river — never as ornament of private power." |
| 12 | "This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties." | "This Covenant, together with the Founding Word and the Articles of Covenant, sets the measure of the bond. No side letter shall silently undo what the Word has made non-negotiable." |

## Of Documents Covered

This voice governs, among others:

- Articles, bylaws, and instruments of stewardship
- Employment and charge-of-office covenants
- Charterparties, bills of lading riders, and house shipping terms where Electrozavod sets the pen
- Notices of breach, removal, and amendment
- Conflict and surplus policies
- Dispute notices and settlement instruments drafted in the house's name

Where Paraguayan statute or maritime convention requires fixed formulary language, keep the formulary where the law demands it, and wrap the house's own undertakings in this register so that the *spirit* remains audible beside the *form*.

---

## Closing Charge to Drafters

Write as if the dead of this land were listening, and as if the children of the river will one day read what you signed.

If a clause would make a sacred person, hide a hand, or set profit above the people — strike it.

If a clause would make power removable, books open, and excellence owed — keep it, and make it sharper.

This is the Charter of the Legal Voice of Electrozavod.

It serves the Founding Word. It does not replace it.
